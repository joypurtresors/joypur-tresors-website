01CC — Cash Credit+

CC — Cash Credit

Discuss working-capital funding connected to the operating cycle of your business.

  • Explain stock, receivables and supplier payment cycles.
  • Discuss assessment of the limit and applicable drawing-power conditions.
  • Ask about reporting, security, review and renewal requirements.

This is a business facility. A salaried profile checklist covers personal income information only; the business borrower’s records are also needed.

02OD — Overdraft+

OD — Overdraft

Discuss an overdraft limit for a permitted business working-capital need.

  • Share the expected gap between receipts and payments.
  • Review security, permitted use and utilisation pattern.
  • Ask about interest, charges, renewal and any limit-reduction schedule.

A profile selection does not establish eligibility. Business-facility assessment also requires the borrower entity’s records.
