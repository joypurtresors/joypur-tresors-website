01Loan Against Property (LAP)+

Loan Against Property (LAP)

Discuss a property-backed loan for a permitted funding purpose with a defined repayment plan.

  • Describe the property, ownership and proposed use of funds.
  • Review income, existing commitments and repayment capacity.
  • Ask about valuation, title checks, tenure, fees and repayment terms.
Required Documents / Checklist

Indicative preparation checklist. The lender will confirm the exact documents and statement periods for your case.

Loan Against Property (LAP) — Salaried

Identity & application
  • Application, photograph and accepted identity / address proofs
  • PAN / tax identification and co-applicant KYC, as applicable
Income & occupation
  • Recent salary slips and salary-credit bank statements
  • Form 16 / income-tax information, as applicable
  • Employment details and existing loan obligations
Category-specific records
  • Property title / ownership chain and relevant property approvals
  • Details of existing charges or loans on the property
  • Proposed end-use details and existing borrowing statements

02MOD — Dropline / Mortgage Overdraft+

MOD — Dropline / Mortgage Overdraft

Explore an overdraft against eligible property, including a reducing-limit (dropline) structure where offered.

  • MOD means Mortgage Overdraft in this guide.
  • An evergreen limit and a dropline limit work differently; a dropline limit reduces on an agreed schedule.
  • Discuss drawings, repayments, limit reductions, interest calculation and renewal conditions before choosing.
