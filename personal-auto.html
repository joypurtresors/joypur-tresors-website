AL — Auto Loan

Discuss finance for a new or used vehicle and the associated repayment plan.

  • Identify the vehicle, purchase price and own contribution.
  • For a used vehicle, discuss age, ownership and valuation requirements.
  • Ask about vehicle security, insurance and total financing costs.
