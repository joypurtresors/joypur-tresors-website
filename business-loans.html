01Business Term Loan+

Business Term Loan

Discuss a planned business investment with a repayment structure suited to the proposal.

  • Describe expansion, equipment or the project being funded.
  • Share estimates, contribution and expected business cash flows.
  • Discuss security, tenure, fees and repayment conditions.

Business borrower / entity records are needed in addition to the selected individual income profile.

Required Documents / Checklist

Indicative preparation checklist. The lender will confirm the exact documents and statement periods for your case.

Business Term Loan — Salaried

Identity & application
  • Application, photograph and accepted identity / address proofs
  • PAN / tax identification and co-applicant KYC, as applicable
Income & occupation
  • Recent salary slips and salary-credit bank statements
  • Form 16 / income-tax information, as applicable
  • Employment details and existing loan obligations
Category-specific records
  • Business constitution, registration and ownership details
  • Financial statements, tax / GST and business banking records
  • Project report, asset quotations or use-of-funds estimates

02Business Working Capital+

Business Working Capital

Discuss the business operating cycle before choosing a funding structure.

  • Map customer receipts, inventory and supplier payments.
  • Discuss current borrowing, seasonal needs and the proposed use of funds.
  • Compare a term facility with OD / CC where relevant.

Salaried income may relate to a co-applicant or guarantor; it does not replace business assessment.
