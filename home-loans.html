01Purchase a New House / Flat / Land+

Purchase a New House / Flat / Land

Explore a ready or under-construction home purchase, or discuss a separate plot-purchase requirement.

  • Tell us whether you are buying a house, flat or vacant residential plot.
  • Discuss purchase price, own contribution and the property stage.
  • A standalone land purchase may require a plot / land loan rather than a standard home-purchase loan; lender rules differ.
Required Documents / Checklist

Indicative preparation checklist. The lender will confirm the exact documents and statement periods for your case.

Purchase a New House / Flat / Land — Salaried

Identity & application

Income & occupation
  • Recent salary slips and salary-credit bank statements

Category-specific records
  • Property title / ownership chain and relevant property approvals
