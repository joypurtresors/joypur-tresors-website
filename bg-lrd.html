01LRD — Lease Rental Discounting+

LRD — Lease Rental Discounting

Discuss funding assessed against rental income from an eligible leased property.

  • Share property ownership, the tenant and the remaining lease period.
  • Discuss rent receipts, rental assignment and existing borrowing.
  • Ask how tenant quality, lease conditions and security affect the proposal.

02BG — Bank Guarantee+

BG — Bank Guarantee

Discuss a financial or performance guarantee required for a contract, tender or other obligation.

  • Identify the beneficiary and the purpose of the guarantee.
  • Share the requested amount, validity, claim period and wording.
  • Ask about margin, security, commission and obligations if the guarantee is invoked.

BG is a guarantee facility, not a cash loan. Requirements depend on the underlying obligation and the lender.
